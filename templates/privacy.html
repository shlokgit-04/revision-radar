Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Revision Radar is a study planner. This page explains what the service stores and why, in plain language.

What we store

  • Account basics: your email address and a salted, hashed password (PBKDF2). We never store your password itself.
  • Your planner workspace: the exam date, chapter list (name, subject, weight, confidence), the generated plan, checkbox ticks, and days marked as slipped. This is what you type in and nothing more.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.
  • We do not track you across other websites, and there are no third-party ad trackers here.

Cookies and sessions

Revision Radar uses signed session tokens (kept in your browser's local storage) so you stay signed in for up to 30 days. Signing out deletes the token from your device.

Deleting your data

You can delete your entire account and all stored workspace data at any time from Settings in the app. Deletion is immediate and permanent. You can also export your workspace as JSON from Settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out through the About page.
